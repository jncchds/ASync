feat(android): All files access: folders anywhere on the phone (task 16)

Android's folder picker refuses a volume's root, Download and Android/...; like
FolderSync, Solid Explorer and Syncthing-fork, the app can now ask for
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (we distribute the APK ourselves).

- Choose folder... explains in one screen and opens the system page for ASync
  (fallback: the general page); declining keeps the SAF picker.
- ASync's own picker over real paths: volumes from StorageManager with their
  labels, volume roots and Download, Android/data and obb left out.
- GuardedTree (Engine): FileSystemTree behind a permission check on every
  call and hidden paths; a revoked permission stops the folder with "All files
  access was turned off", nothing deleted, also in background runs.
- Real paths stored on the server as people read them.
- SAF folders are offered their real path; the sync state is carried over
  (SyncState.Location + ILocalTreeFactory.SamePlace + FolderSyncer.Rebase).
- Settings -> Folders on this phone shows the permission with a button.
- Tests: GuardedTreeTests, AllFilesAccessTests; smoke-android.sh section.
